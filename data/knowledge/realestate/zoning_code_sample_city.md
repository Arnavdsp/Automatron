# Sample City zoning code (synthetic)

Synthetic zoning code for the fictional Sample City. Fictional. It exists so the pre-screen
workflow has something concrete to cite, and mirrors the machine-readable rules used by the
tools.

## Section 3.1 — R-1, single-family residential

Permitted: single-family dwelling, home occupation, accessory structure.
Conditional: religious assembly, school, day care serving more than six.
Not permitted: multi-family dwelling, retail, light industrial.

Minimum lot area 6,000 sq ft. Minimum lot width 55 ft. Front setback 25 ft. Side setback 5 ft
each. Rear setback 20 ft. Maximum height 35 ft and 2.5 storeys. Maximum floor area ratio 0.45.
Maximum lot coverage 40 per cent. Parking 2 spaces per dwelling.

## Section 3.2 — R-2, two-family residential

Permitted: single-family dwelling, two-family dwelling, accessory structure.
Conditional: day care, small assisted living.
Minimum lot area 8,000 sq ft. Minimum lot width 65 ft. Front setback 20 ft. Side setback 6 ft.
Rear setback 20 ft. Maximum height 35 ft. Maximum floor area ratio 0.60. Maximum lot coverage
45 per cent. Parking 1.5 spaces per unit.

## Section 4.1 — C-1, neighbourhood commercial

Permitted: retail under 5,000 sq ft, office, personal service, restaurant without drive-through.
Conditional: restaurant with drive-through, tavern, vehicle fuel sales.
Not permitted: heavy repair, outdoor storage as a principal use.
Minimum lot area 4,000 sq ft. Front setback 10 ft. Side setback 0 ft where abutting C-1, 10 ft
where abutting residential. Rear setback 15 ft. Maximum height 45 ft. Maximum floor area ratio
1.50. Maximum lot coverage 70 per cent. Parking 1 space per 300 sq ft.

## Section 5.1 — MU-1, mixed use

Permitted: multi-family dwelling, retail, office, live-work unit.
Conditional: structured parking as a principal use.
Minimum lot area 10,000 sq ft. Front setback 5 ft. Side setback 0 ft. Rear setback 10 ft.
Maximum height 65 ft and 5 storeys. Maximum floor area ratio 2.50. Maximum lot coverage 80 per
cent. Parking 0.75 spaces per dwelling unit.

## Section 6.1 — Measurement

Floor area ratio is gross floor area divided by lot area. Lot coverage is building footprint
divided by lot area, expressed as a percentage. A dimension exactly equal to a limit complies.

## Section 6.2 — Conditional uses

A conditional use requires a public hearing. Listing a use as conditional is not an
authorisation; it identifies the process that applies.

## Section 6.3 — Unlisted uses

A use not listed in a district is determined by the zoning administrator, who may find it
similar to a listed use. Unlisted does not mean prohibited.
