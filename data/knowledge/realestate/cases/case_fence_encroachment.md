# Fence encroachment found on survey

Synthetic case. Details are fictional.

**Situation.** Survey notes on a residential purchase recorded a fence sitting 1.8 ft inside the
neighbouring parcel along one side.

**What the review showed.** The title commitment carried a general survey exception. The
encroachment was not referenced in any recorded document. The inspection report was unrelated.
Depending on jurisdiction and duration, a long-standing encroachment can raise prescriptive or
boundary questions.

**What the attorney decided.** The item was flagged for attorney review with questions on how
long the fence had stood and whether a boundary line agreement was available. No conclusion was
offered on ownership.

**Lesson.** The survey, not the title commitment, surfaced this. A file without a current survey
would have closed without anyone seeing it.
