# Drive-through in C-1 requiring a hearing

Synthetic case. Details are fictional.

**Situation.** An applicant proposed a restaurant with a drive-through on a C-1 parcel.

**What the pre-screen showed.** Every dimensional standard passed, with floor area ratio at 0.9
against a 1.50 limit. The use itself was listed as conditional in C-1, which requires a public
hearing. Parking computed to the required ratio.

**What the examiner decided.** The result was recorded as hearing likely rather than ready for
examiner, and the brief distinguished the dimensional compliance from the separate question of
whether the conditional use would be granted.

**Lesson.** A project can meet every measurable standard and still not be approvable
administratively. Use permission and dimensional compliance are separate findings.
