# Unreleased mortgage from a prior owner

Synthetic case. Details are fictional.

**Situation.** A purchase in its final week of due diligence.

**What the review showed.** Schedule B-I contained a requirement to release a mortgage recorded
eleven years earlier in the name of an owner two transfers back. The current seller's loan payoff
was listed separately. Nothing in the file showed the older lien had been satisfied.

**What the attorney decided.** Severity was recorded as high, since an unsatisfied requirement
blocks the policy. The proposed next step was a title cure request to the insurer before
closing.

**Lesson.** A Schedule B-I requirement is a live condition. Reading it as historical is a
frequent and consequential mistake.
