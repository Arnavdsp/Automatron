# Security deposit deduction dispute

Synthetic case. Details are fictional.

**Situation.** A departing tenant disputed deductions from a security deposit.

**What the summary showed.** The itemisation listed carpet replacement and repainting. The lease
allows deduction for damage beyond ordinary wear. The tenancy had run four years. Move-in
photographs existed; move-out photographs did not. The deduction notice was sent on a date whose
compliance with the statutory return period could not be determined from the file, because the
applicable period is set by local law.

**What the manager decided.** The brief identified the missing move-out documentation as the
central factual gap, flagged the notice period as needing confirmation against local law rather
than asserting a deadline, and offered mediation and a request for documentation as options.

**Lesson.** Where a legal period governs, the honest output names the placeholder and asks for
confirmation instead of computing a date that looks authoritative and may be wrong.
