# Rear setback shortfall in an R-1 addition

Synthetic case. Details are fictional.

**Situation.** A permit application proposed a rear addition on an R-1 lot.

**What the pre-screen showed.** Lot area 7,200 sq ft against a 6,000 minimum, and lot width 60 ft
against 55, both passing. The proposed rear setback was 17 ft against the required 20, failing.
Floor area ratio computed to 0.41 against a 0.45 limit and lot coverage to 38 per cent against
40, both passing. Height and parking passed.

**What the examiner decided.** The result was recorded as corrections likely, with the single
failing rule cited to its section and the required and proposed values shown side by side.

**Lesson.** Reporting required against proposed for every rule, passing ones included, let the
applicant see exactly what to change and how much room the other dimensions left.
