# Association fence dispute

Synthetic case. Details are fictional.

**Situation.** An owner erected a 6 ft solid fence in a front yard. The association issued a
violation notice and began assessing fines.

**What the summary showed.** The covenants limit front-yard fences to 4 ft and require open
construction. The timeline showed the notice was issued eight days after installation, and that
the first fine was assessed 19 days after the notice, against a 30 day cure period in Article
7.1. The owner's stated position was that a neighbour has a similar fence. The association's
position was that the earlier fence predates the current covenants.

**What the board decided.** The brief set out both positions, the clauses each relied on, the
apparent gap between the fine date and the cure period, and the missing fact of when the
neighbouring fence was installed. Mediation was among the options offered.

**Lesson.** The procedural timing point was found by computing dates against the stated period,
not by weighing who was right.
