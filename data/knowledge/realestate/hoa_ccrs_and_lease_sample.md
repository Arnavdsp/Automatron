# Sample HOA covenants and residential lease (synthetic)

Synthetic documents for the fictional Mill Creek Homeowners Association and a fictional
residential lease. Fictional.

## Mill Creek covenants, selected articles

Article 4.2, accessory structures. No shed, detached garage or other accessory structure may be
erected without prior written approval of the architectural committee. Applications are decided
within 45 days; failure to respond within that period constitutes approval.

Article 4.5, fences. Fences in rear yards may not exceed 6 ft. Fences in front yards may not
exceed 4 ft and must be of open construction. Materials must be approved.

Article 4.9, exterior changes. Paint colours, roofing materials and window replacements require
committee approval against the approved palette.

Article 7.1, enforcement. The association gives written notice of a violation and allows 30 days
to cure before assessing a fine. Fines accrue monthly while the violation continues.

Article 7.4, dispute resolution. Owners and the association shall attempt mediation before
commencing litigation, except where injunctive relief is sought.

## Residential lease, selected clauses

Clause 6, rent and late fees. Rent is due on the first. A late fee applies after the fifth.

Clause 11, repairs. The tenant reports needed repairs in writing. The landlord makes repairs
affecting habitability promptly, and other repairs within 30 days.

Clause 14, entry. The landlord gives 24 hours notice before entry except in an emergency.

Clause 19, security deposit. The deposit is returned within the period required by law, less
itemised deductions for damage beyond ordinary wear.

Clause 22, quiet enjoyment. The tenant shall not unreasonably disturb other residents.

## Note on notice periods

Notice periods for termination, deposit return and cure of a violation are set by state and
local law and override any shorter period in a lease. Any period quoted from these documents is
a placeholder to be confirmed against local law.
