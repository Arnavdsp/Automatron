# Large ticket that breached concentration

Synthetic case. Details are fictional.

**Situation.** A single order with a notional of 480,000 USD was submitted for a client in the
standard segment.

**What the analysis showed.** The notional exceeded the approval threshold on its own. Measured
against the current book the resulting position would have taken the instrument to 27 per cent
of strategy gross exposure, above the 20 per cent concentration limit. One-day 99 per cent
historical value at risk was reported for the proposed position. The instrument was not on the
restricted or watch list.

**What the reviewer decided.** The options put to the approver were to approve at full size, to
reduce size to bring concentration within the limit, to reject, or to escalate. The approver
chose a reduced size. Execution was arranged outside the system.

**Lesson.** A ticket can be within every per-instrument limit and still breach a portfolio-level
constraint. Concentration has to be measured against the book, not the ticket.
