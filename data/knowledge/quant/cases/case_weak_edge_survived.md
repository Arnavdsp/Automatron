# A weak edge that survived scrutiny

Synthetic case. Details are fictional.

**Situation.** A short-horizon mean-reversion signal on index futures was submitted with a
modest annualised Sharpe of 0.82 from a grid of 18 variants and a stated rationale tied to
liquidity provision around the close.

**What the analysis showed.** After costs the Sharpe fell to 0.71. Deflated for 18 trials it was
0.96, just clearing the threshold, helped by the small trial count and a long sample. PBO came
in at 0.18. Four of five walk-forward folds produced positive out-of-sample Sharpe, and the
weakest fold coincided with a period of unusually low volatility.

**What the reviewer decided.** The verdict was recorded as evidence of an edge rather than a
recommendation to deploy. The proposal put to the committee was a paper-trading period with
monitoring of the volatility regime dependence.

**Lesson.** A small honest search with a stated rationale can clear the bar where a large blind
search cannot, even at a much lower headline Sharpe.
