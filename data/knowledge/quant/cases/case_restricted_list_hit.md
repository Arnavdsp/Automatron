# Restricted list hit on a routine ticket

Synthetic case. Details are fictional.

**Situation.** A small ticket, well under the approval threshold, was submitted by a strategy
that trades a broad universe.

**What the analysis showed.** The instrument appeared on the restricted list with an entry dated
that morning. No limit was breached and the notional would otherwise have qualified for
fast-track handling.

**What the reviewer decided.** The gate result was recorded as requiring human approval on the
restricted-list hit alone. Compliance confirmed the restriction was current and the order was
not released.

**Lesson.** Restricted-list screening has to run on every ticket regardless of size. A
size-based fast-track that skips it would have released this order.
