# Strong representment packet for item not received

Synthetic case. Details are fictional.

**Situation.** A 210 USD order was disputed under an item-not-received reason code.

**What the analysis showed.** Carrier tracking showed a full scan history ending in a delivery
confirmation with a signature. The delivery address matched the billing address on file. The
account had six prior undisputed orders from the same device and IP range. Address and card
verification had both passed at authorisation. The completeness score covered every required
evidence type for the category.

**What the specialist decided.** The packet was assembled with exhibits numbered and a draft
rebuttal prepared, for submission through the processor before the stated deadline.

**Lesson.** The likelihood band was reported as a labelled heuristic. The brief did not claim
the dispute would be won.
