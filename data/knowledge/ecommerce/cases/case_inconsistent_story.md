# Claim inconsistent with the order record

Synthetic case. Details are fictional.

**Situation.** A buyer stated in chat that a parcel arrived with two of three items missing and
that the courier left it in the rain on a stated date.

**What the analysis showed.** The date given preceded the carrier's first scan by two days. The
order contained two items, not three. The parcel weight recorded at collection matched the
expected weight for a complete shipment. The transcript contained repeated demands for immediate
refund before any evidence was requested.

**What the specialist decided.** The inconsistencies were listed with the order fields they
contradicted and put to the buyer for clarification before any decision.

**Lesson.** Checking a narrative against structured order data found the discrepancies. The
brief stated what did not match and left the interpretation to the specialist.
