# Reused claim photograph

Synthetic case. Details are fictional.

**Situation.** A buyer filed a damaged-on-arrival claim on a 640 USD espresso machine, three
days after delivery, with two photographs.

**What the analysis showed.** The perceptual hash of one photograph matched an image submitted
with a different claim from the same account eleven months earlier, on a different product. EXIF
data was absent from both. The account had four claims in the preceding twelve months against
nine orders. Order value placed the claim above the high-value threshold, routing it to
specialist review independently.

**What the specialist decided.** The claim was held for verification rather than denied. The
buyer was asked for a short video of the item and its packaging. The account flag was recorded
as a routing signal.

**Lesson.** An image hash match is a strong prompt to verify and a weak basis to conclude. The
brief described it as a signal and proposed a verification step.
