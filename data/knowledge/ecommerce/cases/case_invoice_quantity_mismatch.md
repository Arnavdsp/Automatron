# Appeal with an invoice quantity shortfall

Synthetic case. Details are fictional.

**Situation.** A seller suspended after a counterfeit complaint submitted an appeal with three
supplier invoices.

**What the analysis showed.** The invoices covered 180 units. Platform records showed 447 units
of the same product sold in the period. Two invoice dates fell after the first recorded sale.
The named supplier did not appear on the authorised distributor list. The appeal letter stated
corrective actions but no root cause.

**What the reviewer decided.** The brief listed each inconsistency against the evidence it came
from and proposed specific questions: the source of the remaining units, and the relationship
with the named supplier. The recommendation was to request more information rather than to
uphold or reinstate.

**Lesson.** Arithmetic reconciliation between units invoiced and units sold produced the clearest
question to put to the seller.
