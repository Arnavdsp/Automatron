# Well supported authorised reseller appeal

Synthetic case. Details are fictional.

**Situation.** A seller suspended after an intellectual property notice appealed with a letter of
authorisation and supplier documentation.

**What the analysis showed.** Invoices covered more units than were sold in the period, all
invoice dates preceded the first sale, and the supplier appeared on the authorised distributor
list. The letter of authorisation named the seller and the brand and was within its stated term.
The appeal stated a root cause, corrective actions and preventive measures.

**What the reviewer decided.** The brief recorded the appeal as well supported and put
reinstatement and conditional reinstatement to the reviewer as the two live options, with the
open question of whether the rights holder wished to maintain the notice.

**Lesson.** A neutral summary serves the seller as well as the platform when the evidence is
good.
