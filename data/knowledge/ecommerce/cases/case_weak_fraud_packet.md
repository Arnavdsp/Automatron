# Weak packet on an unauthorised transaction claim

Synthetic case. Details are fictional.

**Situation.** A 95 USD order was disputed as fraudulent and unauthorised.

**What the analysis showed.** The order was placed from a device and IP not seen before on the
account, shipped to an address different from the billing address, with no 3-D Secure
authentication result recorded. Card verification had passed but address verification returned a
partial match. Tracking showed delivery but no signature was captured.

**What the specialist decided.** The options presented were to gather the missing authentication
evidence if the processor could supply it, or to accept liability. Accepting was identified as
the likely outcome given the evidence gaps.

**Lesson.** A packet brief is as useful when it recommends accepting a loss as when it supports
contesting one, provided the gaps are stated plainly.
