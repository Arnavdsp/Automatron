# Low value claim with a clean history

Synthetic case. Details are fictional.

**Situation.** A buyer reported a cracked 24 USD blender jug, two days after delivery, with one
clear photograph showing the damage and the outer box.

**What the analysis showed.** The account was four years old with 38 orders and no prior claims.
The claim fell inside the 30 day window and the category required photographs, which were
supplied. Risk scoring returned a low value with no contributing factors above threshold. Order
value was well under the high-value threshold.

**What the specialist decided.** Fast-track refund, approved at sign-off.

**Lesson.** Most claims are ordinary. A risk process earns its place by routing these quickly
so attention goes to the ones that need it.
