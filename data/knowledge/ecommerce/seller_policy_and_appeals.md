# Sample marketplace seller policy and appeal guidance

Synthetic policy for the fictional marketplace Harbourline. Fictional.

## Enforcement categories

Counterfeit or intellectual property complaint, performance failure, and prohibited item
listing. Enforcement ranges from listing removal through selling-privilege suspension.

## What an appeal should contain

A root cause statement describing what happened and why, in the seller's own account.

Corrective action already taken, with dates.

Preventive measures that would stop recurrence, specific enough to be checked.

Supporting evidence. For an authenticity complaint this normally means supplier invoices
covering the units sold in the relevant period, and where applicable a letter of authorisation
from the rights holder or an authorised distributor.

## How invoices are examined

Invoices are checked for internal arithmetic, for whether the quantities invoiced cover the
units sold, for whether invoice dates precede the first sale date, and for whether the supplier
appears on the authorised distributor list. A shortfall between units invoiced and units sold is
recorded as an inconsistency to put to the seller, not as a finding of counterfeiting.

## Decisions

Reinstatement, conditional reinstatement, a request for more information and upholding the
enforcement are decided by a trained reviewer. Analysis prepares a neutral summary of strengths,
weaknesses and open questions.
