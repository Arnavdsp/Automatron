# Sample return and refund policy

Synthetic policy for the fictional marketplace Harbourline. Fictional, written to give the
workflow something concrete to cite.

## Return window

Most items may be returned within 30 days of delivery. Electronics carry a 14 day window.
Perishable goods, personalised items, opened hygiene products and digital downloads are not
returnable except where the item arrived damaged or not as described.

## Claim categories and evidence

Damaged on arrival: photographs of the item and of the outer packaging are required. Claims
filed more than 7 days after delivery require an explanation of the delay.

Not as described: photographs plus a description of the difference from the listing.

Missing item: the claim is checked against the carrier's weight record for the parcel where one
exists.

Item not received: the carrier tracking record is checked first. Where tracking shows delivery,
the claim proceeds only with a statement from the buyer and is routed to a specialist.

Changed mind: return shipping is paid by the buyer and the item must be unused and in original
packaging.

## High value handling

Claims on orders at or above 500 USD are routed to specialist review regardless of category and
are not eligible for automatic refund.

## Repeat claims

An account with more than three claims in a rolling 12 months, or a claim rate above 15 per cent
of orders, is flagged for specialist review. A flag is a routing signal and is not evidence of
wrongdoing.

## Decisions

Refunds, replacements and denials are decided by a trained specialist. Automated checks prepare
the evidence and route the claim; they do not decide it.
