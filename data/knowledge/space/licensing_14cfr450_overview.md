# US launch and reentry licensing, 14 CFR Part 450

Summary written for this project. Verify against the official source before relying on it.

Part 450 is the consolidated Federal Aviation Administration rule governing licences for launch
and reentry operations conducted from or returning to the United States, and for US entities
operating elsewhere. It replaced a set of separate launch and reentry regulations with one
performance-based framework.

## What a licence covers

A licence authorises an operator to conduct a described operation from a described site within a
stated period. The application identifies the vehicle, the trajectory, the site, and the
operator's safety organisation.

## Principal elements of an application

Policy review, which considers foreign policy and national security implications.

Payload review, unless the payload is otherwise licensed by another agency, such as a
communications payload authorised by the Federal Communications Commission or a remote sensing
payload licensed by the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration.

Safety review, the substantial part. It covers the flight safety analysis, including debris,
toxic and far-field blast hazards where applicable, and requires the operator to show that
expected casualty from the operation falls within the regulatory criteria.

Environmental review under the National Environmental Policy Act.

Financial responsibility, meaning a maximum probable loss determination and the liability
insurance or demonstration of financial responsibility that follows from it.

## Timelines

The rule sets a review period that begins once an application is accepted as complete. Accepting
an application as complete is itself a step, and incomplete submissions restart the clock.
Pre-application consultation is the normal way to reduce that risk, and operators are encouraged
to engage well before filing.

## What this means for a decision brief

A brief prepared for a licensing packet should list each required element, state whether the
mission profile supplies it, and identify gaps. It should not assert that a licence will be
granted, that a filing is complete, or that a timeline is assured. Novel activities that do not
fit the launch and reentry framework need regulatory counsel rather than a checklist.
