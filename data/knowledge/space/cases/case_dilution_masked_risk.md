# Probability dilution masked a real conjunction

Synthetic case. Details are fictional.

**Situation.** A conjunction against a small debris object reported Pc of 8.0e-8, far below the
monitoring threshold, and was initially set aside.

**What the analysis showed.** The debris object had been tracked only twice in the preceding
fortnight and its along-track sigma exceeded 4 km. A dilution check scaling the combined
covariance between 0.1 and 10 found the maximum reachable Pc was 2.3e-4, more than three orders
of magnitude above the reported figure. The low number came from uncertainty so large that the
probability mass had spread out, not from a comfortable miss distance.

**What the operator decided.** The conjunction was returned to active monitoring and tracking
was requested. Improved tracking narrowed the covariance and the refined Pc settled at 4.7e-5,
below the manoeuvre threshold but well inside the monitoring band.

**Lesson.** A low Pc means little without a covariance quality check. Dilution testing should
run on every screening, not only on conjunctions that already look concerning.
