# Servicing mission with no established framework

Synthetic case. Details are fictional.

**Situation.** An operator prepared a licensing packet for a mission to rendezvous with and
refuel a third-party geostationary satellite.

**What the review showed.** The launch segment mapped cleanly onto the launch and reentry rules
and the communications payload onto the domestic satellite licensing route. The servicing
activity itself did not map onto either. Questions arose about authorisation of the proximity
operation, liability allocation between the servicer and the client operator, and which entity
holds responsibility for the combined object during mated operations. Requirements generation
produced a checklist with several items marked as having no established framework.

**What the operator decided.** The packet was assembled as a draft for regulatory counsel, with
the unmapped items listed explicitly as open questions rather than gaps to be filled from a
template. Early engagement with the regulator was scheduled before any filing.

**Lesson.** Where an activity falls outside an existing regime, the useful output is a
well-organised set of open questions for counsel, not a checklist that implies the activity fits.
