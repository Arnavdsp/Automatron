# Spectrum overlap found late in planning

Synthetic case. Details are fictional.

**Situation.** A planned Ku-band downlink for a low Earth orbit imaging constellation was
checked against the registry of existing systems.

**What the review showed.** Two operators held assignments overlapping the proposed band once
the full bandwidth was accounted for, one in a similar orbital regime. The overlap had been
missed earlier because the initial check used the centre frequency alone rather than the centre
frequency plus the bandwidth on each side.

**What the operator decided.** Draft coordination letters were prepared for both affected
parties, setting out the proposed technical parameters and inviting technical discussion. The
mission timeline was revised to reflect coordination as the critical path rather than an
administrative step at the end.

**Lesson.** Band overlap checks must use the occupied bandwidth, not the centre frequency.
Discovering an affected party late converts coordination from a parallel activity into a
schedule driver.
